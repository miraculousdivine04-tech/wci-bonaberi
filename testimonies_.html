Deliverance via the Anointing Oil

I joined this church a month ago. During one of the services, an announcement was made concerning WOFBI. So, I made up my mind to register and participate. We began on Monday the 16th of March 2026 with the course New Creation Fundamentals. The light that I caught from the teaching was very powerful. On Tuesday the following day, while I was preparing to come for the lectures, a centipede entered my ear. I started crying, it was so painful, I could feel it moving inside my ear and I kept shouting for help. My mother rushed to me with the anointing oil; she prayed and administered it on my ear. Behold, immediately the oil was administered, the centipede came out on its own. I thank God for delivering me from the attack of the devil.

Sis Loveline, Winners' Chapel Int'l Bonaberi

Financial Favor

I have been looking for a way to buy a new phone for my business. And on Sunday, our Daddy in the house, Pastor Ernest, declared that we will experience financial favor. I said “Amen” and held unto that word. A lady in the United States of America wrote to me, we don't know each other personally, but we are in a prayer group together on WhatsApp. Without me asking anything, she said, the Lord has placed in her heart to bless me. She sent me some money and asked me to buy an iPhone. Now, I have a new phone for my business and I return all the glory to God.

Sis Loveline, Winners' Chapel Int'l Bonaberi

Supernatural Provision Through Prophetic Prayers

My testimony is on behalf of a friend; she's like a sister to me. She was faced with challenges, so I brought her to meet our Pastor in his office, Pastor Ernest. She explained her difficulties to him, and he prayed for her. One evening, she called me to say she was sick, asking me to bring her some food. Which I did. The next morning, after I had prepared to go to my business place. Immediately I closed the door of my house to be on my way, I heard a voice asking me to go and check on my sick friend. I decided to ignore the voice because I was really in need of money and had to go to work. But, all the way to the road side, that voice kept talking to me and saying the same thing. I finally obeyed. I went back to the home, took some food, and went to check on her. When I got to her room, there was blood everywhere. The neighbors told me she was bleeding all night, so she had to be rushed to the hospital. When I got to the hospital, the doctor said she was suffering from fibroid, and that one of them blew up and that's what caused the bleeding. But glory to God, they stopped the bleeding.

The doctor said the next step is to operate on her and it will cost a very huge amount of money because it's a major operation. I was under much pressure because he said, the effects of the injection given to her in order to stop the bleeding will soon dissipate, and as soon as that happens, the bleeding will start again and she does not have enough blood left. After hearing that, her blood pressure started rising, because she didn't have the money needed.

I took my phone while crying, and called Pastor Ernest. As soon as I explained what was happening, he said I should give her the phone. Which I did. He didn't pray for healing, but he said, “everything needed for this operation to be successful, God will provide it”. He also prayed for angels to go ahead of her into the theater room, and that she will come out alive, sooner than we expect. We all shouted, “Amen”.

Later, I wanted to call Daddy again, and explain to him the whole situation in details because the first time, I was crying while talking. Suddenly, a doctor stepped in and asked to see my friend. The next thing I saw, was that her dresses were brought to me. When I asked “what happened?”, they said she is already in the theater room undergoing the operation. Asked how that was possible, they said the doctor who asked to see her stepped in and paid all the bills.

I told her junior sister to stay in the hospital, while I go home to prepare some food so that after the surgery, she could eat and recover. But while I was still talking with her junior sister, a doctor called for me and told me to prepare her bed, because they were done with the operation. Immediately, I remembered Pastor Ernest had declared she will come out alive and sooner than we expect. I return all the glory to God for this wonderful works.

Sis Stephanie Ike, Winners' Chapel Int'l Bonaberi
